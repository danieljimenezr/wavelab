Assay An assay is the test that decides whether an ore is gold or pyrite. This does the same to a trading strategy: five tests that almost nothing passes. It does not tell you when to buy — it tells you whether what you believe works holds up.

Upload a CSV with a date column and a signal column. It takes ISO dates or epochs, and signals like 1/0/-1, long/flat/short or buy/sell. A position is held until you change it.
Add a price column and it is validated against your asset — equities, FX, whatever crypto. Without one, our BTC series is used.

Write when to be long and, if you want, when to be short. When neither holds, you are out of the market.
